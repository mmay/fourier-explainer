An interactive introductionFourier Transforms, Visually

Any signal — a sound, an image, a stock price — can be rebuilt out of nothing but simple sine waves. That single idea is the Fourier transform. This is a hands-on walk through what it actually means, one dial at a time.

Who this is for: you don't need calculus to get the intuition, but the real formulas are included at each step for when you want them. Every chapter has something you can drag, and nothing here is a video — it's all live and running in your browser.
